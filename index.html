About

Hello! I'm Rithwik V Kumar, a second year Electronics and Communication Engineering student currently in my 3rd semester. I enjoy working at the intersection of embedded systems, IoT, electronics and software, and I like turning what I learn in class into practical projects.

On the studious side, I'm interested in understanding how systems actually work, from microcontrollers, sensors and communication protocols to firmware, circuit design and the software that ties everything together. I learn best by building, debugging and improving things rather than stopping at theory.

Outside the academic side, I'm pretty hands-on and curious. I enjoy Linux, gaming, cars, photography, and taking random ideas and seeing if I can actually build them. A lot of my projects follow the same mindset: learn something, mess with it, break it, fix it, and make it useful.
